# Phase 3C.2 results: shared capacity and stable reservation

## Question and result

Can exactly authorized fixed-chain services conserve a reusable slot yet lose
finite completion, and what must a retained composition contract preserve?
Yes: equal full base transport/physical state and equal pool occupancy can hide
opposite completion possibilities when the original holding that slot differs.
This is a research-model result for A→B→C, not a runtime defect or a new general
deadlock phenomenon. The controller's inspected primary background is recorded
in [the literature note](evidence/manuscript/primary-literature.md).

The [frozen contract](00-contract.md) separates permanent exact permission,
sufficient cumulative expenditure E and reusable capacity Q. The old effect
resources remain distinct. Roots and original membership are immutable; apply
requires an admitted held original and the service guard, then atomically
applies once, retains the actual opaque result and releases that original's Q.
Consumption needs no Q. Crash and durable-ACK receipt release never release Q.
Recoverable owner interruption, atomic trusted root updates, intact durable
custody and fixed identities/topology/policies are premises.

## Negative boundary and conditional positive argument

With serial B under early/shared-1, both originals can be W/admitted. AB holding
the only slot blocks itself on BC, while BC cannot reserve; a matched BC-holder
state can finish BC then AB. Genuine captured prefixes establish equality of
the entire base records, with no post-state rewriting. Ready-now does not fix
this: reserve AB while BC=U, then BC commit invalidates B's service guard.
Authorization, conservation and authentic custody hold in these adverse states.

Stable-ready requires a permanently servicing callee or all of that callee's
outgoing originals D (vacuously for C). With immutable policies and absorbing
D, a reachable holder stays ready until application. Apply existing holders,
consume applied work through real custody, then finish BC before AB. This is
an existential finite-suffix source argument under the premises, not fairness,
necessity, policy optimality or inevitable completion.

The [source argument](01-argument.md) separately gives projection and a finite
lift of each enabled **resource-aware** action from every reachable concrete
representative. Preparation recovers endpoints/routes custody without changing
holds or another original's stage. Hence concrete and resource-aware abstract
CanComplete coincide under those premises. Completion means both originals D.
The argument is not mechanized; finite probes do not establish its universal
quantifier. The old external observation erases reserve-capacity, but the old
AB-before-BC-commit trace cannot lift under stable-ready with serial B. Advancing
BC adds visible events. No old-q backward-lifting or unrestricted trace
equivalence follows from the stronger policy.

Static joins retain full bindings, endpoint policies, exact root identities,
limits and memberships. Equal root references identify one pool; limits are
not summed. Both parenthesizations yield the same closed fixed relation.
Dynamic holds live in one shared relation, not a union of independent local
histories. Open-component substitution and arbitrary-topology associativity
remain unestablished.
