## Closure-relevant judgement

1. The early work gives real explicit contracts, concrete adverse schedules, finite models and implemented fragments; it does not merely propose a plan. Bounded closure is supportable for those named slices.
2. Unavailable positive new-invocation/ownership writers are characterized restrictions of the admitted fragment. They do not automatically demand new implementation for a research closure whose gate permits counterexamples and fragments. They **do** prevent positive claims across real generation/revision-changing transitions; false claims and refused Retry do not replace such evidence.
3. On the evidence inspected here, the most direct original-gate questions to carry into the full-series assessment are cancellation after physical admission and B's own effect/receipt window. Avoid silently using “revocation” for all cancellation or “two-agent” for symmetric crash coverage. These may be discharged by later slices, but 3B.1–3B.4 alone do not establish them in the inspected raw records.
4. General owner transfer, arbitrary contexts, multihop, shared-resource interference and more-agent closure remain outside the demonstrated domain. Their absence is not by itself evidence that an explicitly bounded Phase 3B research program failed its original fragment-based exit condition.
