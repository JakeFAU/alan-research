## 1. Domain and distinct relations

Fix one Store namespace N, two initial logical Runs A=(a,1), B=(b,1), immutable retained programs, original root memberships/revisions and complete permission rows. A services BA while unpaid; B services AB only after consuming BA's genuine result. One trusted current Store owner, one-hop grant issuance, ordinary raw programs and the declared endpoint contract bound the positive program family. This restriction on the fixture does not narrow the contract's supported per-artifact semantics; authored compatibility K01/K02 remains mandatory.

A prefix P is an ordered composition-fact prefix plus its causal source records. History rows/observations loaded from a later snapshot can justify P only when their required predecessor facts already occur in P. Keep three coordinate sorts distinct: c/e.Sequence are **per-logical-Run reduction sequences**; g.DecisionSequence is the **lifecycle-result decision sequence**; causal fact ordering uses **composition decision_order**. Locate the lifecycle/execution/reservation facts to establish grant/selection/service-before-decision; do not compare g.DecisionSequence directly to c/e or to composition order. Never compare numbers of different sorts. Canonical original operation IDs are JSON `[subject,generation,sequence,ordinal]`; namespaces/program identities remain in a separate enclosing identity record.

Let q be a committed ordinary original request, o its canonical ordinary-message observation, c a real selecting history row, g the selected accepted grant, e an authentic ordinal-zero external/adapter service, and D the fixed first-decision boundary. The following definitions are distinct; in particular H does not contain present-day permission:

* **S(P,q,o,c)**: q's canonical descriptor/index and committing execution agree; its source authorization, complete route permission and authentic accepted admission precede selection. o is exactly `identity("observation","message",q.ID)` in the actual recipient Run; observation/index/descriptor/provenance/source generation/content agree. Some supplying admission with its actual transport ID has Accepted=true, Bound equal to that Run and ObservationID=o.ID. The actual same-Run history at c selects o, its program reduction is genuine, `o.consumed_sequence=c.Sequence>0`, and its execution fact is in P. Multiple valid transport attempts may share o; ACK completion is not required. Application registers and payload request_id strings are not q's identity.
* **H_D(P,q,o,c,g,e)**: S holds; e's execution is in P and in the same recipient generation as c, with preserved recipient revision and `c.Sequence < e.Sequence`. e's immutable selector chooses g; genuine committed lifecycle issuance and accepted g precede D; g names exactly q.ID and e.ID, both authentic roots, holder/recipient generations/revisions and complete `(Kind,Target,Operation,Obligation)` action. Original q precedes issuance. Admission/source authorization precede c, c execution precedes e execution, and e execution precedes D. Grant acceptance may fall before or after c, or after e commitment but before D. H_D refers to these historical coordinates, not a newly selected decision time.
* **V(P,e)**: the exact retained path has current holder/recipient generation, revision and membership, unrevoked root, current source/scope eligibility and current physical owner epoch at the decision or gate in question. The current gate reloads the original operation, first decision and debit; attempt/reservation agreement alone cannot supply authority. Normal holder completion alone does not revoke; no TTL is added. Retirement, closure, revision/generation change and explicit monotone revocation have their actual inherited effects.
* **R(P,e)**: the relevant root MaxEffects/MaxInFlight reservation/gate conditions and execution/byte/runner headroom hold. Permission and reservation allowance are different predicates. An old allowed decision is immutable; another allowed decision, charge or fresh physical opportunity does not follow from H_D.

**Admission order limitation.** AdmissionDescriptor/AdmissionResult has no composition decision_order or transport timestamp. “Admission precedes selection” denotes the causal prerequisite supplied by the genuine atomic admission writer and actual selecting execution, not a nonexistent recorded global ordinal. Source authorization and execution have composition facts; admission must join their authentic descriptors and selecting observation. At least one accepted supplying admission made o available before c; additional same-logical-message transports can occur later and need not all precede c. A final snapshot cannot reconstruct every transport's wall-clock order. The raw checker must report its justified causal linearization and cannot prove historical authenticity against a fully coherent unsigned rewrite. Arbitrary valid transport IDs and multiple admissions do not justify inventing a time field, assuming canonical transport names or changing the journal.

When a service or grant is not yet committed, S can already hold but H_D is not asserted. A planned service locator is a construction reference, not a fictitious emitted intent. A first refusal before grant or selection remains the original refusal. Later P does not replace D. Missing/future selection in an otherwise valid prefix is a policy refusal; contradictory claimed selection, missing causal evidence for an existing claimed selection, or incompatible descriptors are corruption. Neither a successful endpoint nor a checker error repairs a contradiction.

**Quantified temporal consequences.** For every fixed tuple q/o/c/g/e/D satisfying H_D and every permitted extension P→P', the same historical tuple satisfies H_D at P'. Conversely, for any authentic c with c.Sequence≥e.Sequence, no extension preserving c and e can make it satisfy H_D. A decision delayed beyond c does not change that arithmetic. An accepted grant after D cannot satisfy grant-before-D. Revocation after D leaves H_D true while making the applicable fresh V false. These are logical consequences of the definitions and the preservation argument below, not observations of v2 execution.

## 3. Permitted stretches and conditional authority claim

For each fixed program, define a finite instruction slice between authentic q selection and exact e commitment. Its transitive live read set L includes every operand/control dependency that determines e's kind/target/generation/obligation/payload/authority, the service-result matching operation, authentic reply request/service/result fields, and the subsequent principal result-consumption checks. Preservation includes explicit instruction metadata and any Updates at emission; it is not limited to obvious Data references. The positive scratch domain has no collection operations, dynamic branch targets, lifecycle effects or additional service emissions. All paths reach the designated service and preserve the principal custody suffix.

Allowed local instructions are existing `set` with bounded Literal or `Ref` operands and forward `branch` instructions. Set/copy destinations must be disjoint from L; copying a live value into scratch is allowed if size limits still hold. There is no copy opcode. Disallow Updates in inserted instructions. Branch predicates may inspect scratch, but both finite paths preserve L and lead to the same service after the same number of committed reductions for the fixed-grant constructor. Unequal-length paths require a genuine actual-locator grant or leave this family. Merely being forward does not establish that the service is reached.

For the standard raw fixture, service target/profile/delta/authority and reply request_id are literals; service_operation, service_accepted, mutation, total and paid-dependent control/results are live. `peer_request` and `peer_claim` are written by the original receive but not read by its service/reply suffix in `examples/delegation/reciprocal.go:128–159`. Thus P11 may overwrite **peer_request only**, after selection, while preserving all live operands. This is a source-supported dead-register test, not a general dataflow theorem. A later same-peer receive P10 must likewise preserve the live set; q2 remains a distinct unpaid original even if its payload duplicates q.

Readiness selection is not an unconditionally silent step: the ready message must genuinely be committed, source-authorized, delivered/admitted and selected under the receive match in the eligible Run. Its Bind writes (`grant_data` here) preserve L; its payload does not replace e's fixed GrantID. Eventual delivery/selection is an added progress premise. Waiting forever for ready leaves S true but yields no e and no completion.

**Authority statement A (conditional proof sketch).** For every fixed-prefix service satisfying H_D and the same complete path/tuple as the reference construction, if the inherited source/authority checks, V at D and sufficient R all hold, replacing immediate predecessor selection with the contract's exact earlier selection introduces no request-binding refusal. The predicate reads retained q/o/c/g/e, not current scratch/latest receive; the footprint and H_D establish each historical conjunct. No alternate tuple/root/path is added. At every fresh gate repeat current V/R and original-operation checks; the local-history conclusion alone says nothing about those checks. Removal of any premise has a frozen adverse control. In particular, normal holder completion differs from retirement/revocation, and an allowed old opportunity or retained response can remain meaningful after revocation without licensing a fresh opportunity.
