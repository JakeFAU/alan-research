## Independent boundaries

The checker reconstructs original q, canonical observation o, actual selection c, accepted grant g, exact service e, first decision, fresh physical gate and result lineage from exported raw tables. It imports no runtime authorization, executor, Reduce or Replay implementation. V1 retains adjacency semantics; v2 requires the real selection with `0 < c < e`, exact identity/revision and supplying admission/source-authorization causality.

Principal originals are declared explicitly. Separate q2 remains auxiliary when payloads or numeric totals coincide. Raw validity, endpoint contact/application evidence and supported projection to the old model are separate verdicts. Unsupported projection never excuses invalid raw evidence. `CheckRaw` without an explicit declaration marks v2 principal custody unchecked; `CheckBindingDirectory` and the `-binding-case` CLI perform full case qualification.

Lifecycle history is evaluated at its actual prefix. Later retirement does not rewrite earlier authorization. Current eligibility is checked independently against final subject state. Real cancellation, retirement, Retry refusal and grant revocation are represented; normal holder cancellation is not treated as grant revocation. No unsupported generation/revision-changing public writer is invented.
