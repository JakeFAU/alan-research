## Three separate prospective claims

### H — historical binding persistence

Let P be an authentic retained prefix in the candidate profile, q a committed original message, o its legitimately admitted recipient observation, c its committed selecting reduction, g the exact accepted grant and e the authentic emitted service. Define H(P,q,o,c,g,e) to require:

1. q's immutable original identity, source generation/revision, route permission and accepted admission are valid; admission binds o to A's actual logical generation, and o is exactly the observation of q.
2. c is an actual A history row selecting o, and `o.consumed_sequence == c.sequence`; the history row and observation descriptor/index agree. Their execution/admission causal predecessors exist by c's prefix, not merely in final storage.
3. e is an actual ordinal-zero service intent of A in that same logical generation and `0 < c.sequence < e.sequence`. This strict comparison remains true even when e's first authority decision happens much later. Selecting q after e's commitment cannot repair e.
4. g is a genuinely accepted committed lifecycle operation naming exactly q and e. Its holder, recipient/root coordinates, complete permission tuple, recipient revision and selected grant ID match the retained service selector. Grant acceptance must precede the relevant decision; historical replay cannot use a later grant.

Separate the selection fact S(P,q,o,c) from H so a prefix before grant acceptance or service emission can still retain S. H is a provenance relationship, not current authority. Revocation, terminal status, logical retirement or later-generation creation do not erase the historical truth that an earlier generation selected q. They can invalidate a new use of that history.

Proposed conditional persistence statement: if H holds at P, and P→P' only appends valid committed events or performs validated same-Store owner renewal without altering old evidence, H still holds at P'. Before e exists, S remains while a planned service locator is only a future reference. Preserve namespace, original IDs, programs, logical generations and old grants/decisions across reopening; epochs can advance. H is false or unresolved when the selection never committed, causal evidence is missing/contradictory, or a claimed selection belongs to another generation. Never reconstruct missing evidence from successful endpoint output.

A source-level induction can establish preservation by each declared transition constructor, conditional on the transaction/append and validation invariants. Concrete SIGKILL/reopen evidence separately tests those premises. Finite enumeration alone establishes only the enumerated instances.

### A — current-authority preservation under additional premises

Let V(P,e,path) include the exact retained selected path, authentic accepted grant, current unrevoked original holder tuple/root, current generation/revision/membership, appropriate serving-source openness/eligibility and owned physical epoch. Let R(P,e) mean applicable reservation/admission resource conditions hold.

The useful statement is: under H, V and sufficient R, permitted finite bookkeeping/readiness steps that preserve the exact e, selected path, program, complete tuple, logical identities and eligibility do not introduce an additional request-binding refusal. This does not assert permission or reservation allowance if another predicate fails. Nested permission allowance and reservation allowance remain distinct (`check.go:756–780`). A fresh gate rechecks current V from the original service/selected path; an old allowed reservation or historical S does not furnish current V (`check.go:793–807`).

Revocation-after-selection is a required negative with S/H retained and the new gate refused. Changed holder/recipient generations or revisions, retirement, serving closure, exhausted resources, altered service/tuple and no longer matching selected path are outside the positive invariance premise. Normal delegator completion remains subject to the existing contract: it does not by itself revoke the grant. Existing admitted opportunities and retained results retain their own meanings; neither is a license for another physical action. A refused original stays refused after a later grant.

### C — restricted correspondence and progress

Use a separately named relation between a candidate runtime prefix, a candidate selection-aware auxiliary state (if needed), and the unchanged S2/LateGrant state. The old model has no selection coordinate: `research/authorityservice/model.go:378–413` enables authorization after admission and allows separate late grant retention. Thus deleting adjacency does not establish equivalence with every old-model prefix. The candidate still requires actual selection and exact program/operation binding.

Restrict the positive relation to two designated principal originals AB and BA, A servicing/B serial, one-hop accepted grant, fixed complete action mapping, no revocation, sufficient finite resources, no sticky first refusal, exact original/result custody and known atomic-result prefixes with one relevant physical exposure. At the discriminating authorization prefix require q selected before e, g accepted before decision and V/R. A positive candidate would match the old model's Allowed/Delegated edge here; old v1 must retain its refusal and mismatch.

Visible vocabulary: principal request commit, authenticated send/admit (a justified causal linearization, not invented global timing), accepted grant retention, original first authority decision, fresh gate/refusal, known effect result/apply abstraction, authentic result-bearing reply send/admit/record and each principal caller's actual consumption. Every projected event retains its raw original ID and source fact. Physical application/contact, retention, receipt selection, caller reply selection, ACK completion, terminal principals and quiescence remain separate facts.

Permitted hidden local transitions must have an explicit relation clause, not a blanket omission: they preserve H/V and the pending original-service correspondence, produce no newly visible service, retain bounded costs, and have a finite progress measure. Forward-only branch targets and a fixed finite slice can supply a decreasing distance-to-service rank. Readiness receives are blocking observations and need a separately justified eventual delivery/selection premise; they are not costless guaranteed progress. A changed branch target that skips the service cannot stutter under the progress relation.

The restricted result should initially be (i) prefix agreement on the actual restored authorization, (ii) concrete complete reciprocal executions and (iii) existential safe completion within the declared finite auxiliary graph. An induction over fixed finite instruction stretches can support a source-family binding claim, not inevitable scheduler termination or all-model-history realization. Old `QueryProgress` explicitly records this distinction (`progress.go:48–55`); prior counts/results at `04-results.md:121–160` are historical evidence, not reruns here.

## Required mutation sensitivity

Alongside coherently rehashed raw-evidence mutations, actually mutate checker source to remove (1) strict selection-before-service, (2) exact original lookup in favor of latest same-peer receive, (3) generation equality and (4) revocation-at-fresh-gate. Each mutant must fail an assertion on a specially applicable fixture, not crash/build-fail. Positive q→q' control kills false refusal by latest-receive substitution; selected-only-q' control kills false acceptance. A future-selection attack must keep grant/effect/program/remaining chronology coherent enough to isolate the strict ordering check.

Also exercise exact-service, full tuple, namespace/root/revision, historical grant-order, original/gate reconstruction and no fallback substitutions. Rehash dependent descriptors densely. Incomplete prefixes need explicit valid-incomplete or semantic-invalid verdicts; panic/exit2 is apparatus failure. Keep old generator's known incomplete-prefix diagnostic limitation recorded separately (`04-results.md:408–413`), and do not infer negative semantic evidence from it.

## Mandatory identity, resource and cost sidecars

Identity sidecar per execution: namespace, storage/profile version, program hashes, logical holder/recipient/root IDs, original q/o/c/g/e IDs and sequence/order coordinates, actual selection references, full tuples/revisions, serving root, source epochs at each gate, owner identities and snapshot digests. Within an interrupted execution IDs must be identical. Across separate padded provisions provide an explicit partial bijection for designated semantic roles, retaining original full IDs and changed programs/version bytes. Do not claim byte-identical histories or permission-algebra isomorphism.

Resource sidecar: actual committed step count, observation/selection count, bytes of retained program/projections, original reservations and charges, MaxEffects/MaxInFlight occupancy and headroom, pending result/ACK/terminal obligations, endpoint contacts/applications and exposure ordinals. Do not fabricate a step-budget debit the runtime does not implement: runtime `program.go:99–102` exposes byte limits; Store also checks sequence/epoch overflow (`store.go:835–836,680–681`). Declare each actual limiting mechanism rather than calling all costs effects.

Cost guard: predeclare lengths 0/1/8/32, same toolchain/machine/operation shape, separately measure construction, first-decision validation, fresh-gate validation, Replay and Open; retain raw timings/allocations or query evidence and exact source pair. This is a regression guard, not a general performance result. Baseline already scans composition facts in `request_authority.go:177–217` and all admissions in `:464–477`; do not claim current total validation is constant time. The new selection join should avoid an additional full-history scan where exact q→admission/observation→consumed-sequence→history coordinates suffice. Admission transport-key determinism/uniqueness must be checked against the actual public writer before claiming direct indexing; `messages.go:191` exposes a supplied transportID and `:365–378` keys by trusted domain plus that transport identity.
