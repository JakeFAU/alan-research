## Six disposition answers

### 1. Did retained binding remove immediate selection?

**Yes, tested in the declared v2 family.** Automatic P02-v2 selects original q=B1 at A2, ready at A3, and emits the exact granted service e=A4. Both principal result chains complete. Equal-length P02-v1 keeps the same service sequence position but refuses `authority_request_not_admitted`, with no endpoint contact. This discriminates policy from a stale service locator. Successful finite local set/copy stretches and both equal-length forward branches also retain q's earlier selection; other same-peer selection and dead-register overwrite do not replace it. The declared semantic family requires genuine q/o/c before exact e, unchanged live operands and identity, adequate resources and valid authority. An arbitrary inserted instruction is outside that conclusion.

### 2. What evidence and representation sufficed?

**Existing causal records suffice for this implementation; explicit semantic versioning was necessary.** Lookup starts from the accepted grant's exact `OriginalRequestID`, joins the canonical observation o, accepted supplying admission, source authorization and actual selecting history c, and requires `0 < c.Sequence < e.Sequence` in the same logical generation. The grant must name exact e, complete permission tuple, roots, generations and revisions and be accepted before the relevant decision. Historical validation uses the appropriate prefix. Every fresh physical gate separately checks the original selected path, immutable first decision/debit, current source/root eligibility, revocation and owner epoch.

The existing witness records request observation and selecting sequence. No binding object, table, registry, mutable last-request register or second budget ledger is added. `alan/request-authority/v2` uses formats 15/16 and one nonunique, ascending BINARY `admissions_observation_id` index. V1 and formats 13/14 keep their original meaning; old readers reject the new formats and a coherent downgrade cannot hide v2 interpretation. Store metadata `alan/1d/v1` is distinct from the supported raw/authored/collection artifact versions (`alan/1d/v1`, `alan/1e/v1`, `alan/1g/v1`). Exact authored public K01/K02 parents use formats 13/15; separate disposable-copy promotion to 14/16 appends losing receipt evidence through the existing internal writer. That ancillary comparison establishes no public conflict-production schedule.

Per-Run c/e sequences, lifecycle grant decision sequences and global composition-fact order are different coordinates. Admissions have no global timestamp: precedence is a justified causal linearization under genuine atomic-writer/selection assumptions, not measured transport order or authenticity against a coherent unsigned rewrite.

**Conditional proof sketches plus finite tested implementation properties; no new universal or mechanized theorem.** S/H selection/binding persistence follows an unmechanized induction over the declared append/conflict/single-assignment write footprints, assuming trusted ownership, atomic transactions, immutable artifacts and no hostile database rewriting. Authority preservation additionally assumes current validity, resource headroom and unchanged complete path/tuple. Permitted set/copy destinations avoid live service/result/custody dependencies; branches have finite equal executed length and reach the same exact granted service. Open validates then renews only open-run epochs; it does not select q or mint g. These sketches range over declared finite stretches with those premises, rather than infer induction from the tested lengths.

### 6. Which correspondence gaps remain?

**Open outside restricted C.** C checks particular forward visible traces in the nonrevoking, known-result, single-exposure, two-principal A-servicing/B-serial domain, with actual selection before service and a full identity/cost/pending sidecar. Its reverse claim is only a conditional named completion suffix under eventual eligible scheduling/readiness/result delivery, stable authority, adequate resources and no adverse loss. An abstract admitted-but-unselected request may authorize earlier than the runtime; there is no reverse match for every model move, bisimulation, all-state completion or inevitable termination.

Whole-Store SIGKILL/reopen validates concrete continuity/current gates; it is not a chronological mapping to independently crashing A/B. Separate accepted grant plus revocation is absent from the frozen LateGrant/Revocable configuration combination. Applied-but-unrecorded or unknown effects and repeated idempotent exposures do not match atomic model application/receipt. Extra originals, rejected issuance, early sticky refusals, ordinary lifecycle steps and unsupported artifact/instruction histories likewise retain explicit projection exclusions. Valid raw evidence, endpoint evidence and supported projection are separate verdicts; unsupported C never excuses invalid raw evidence. Binding proves permission for this exact authentic request/service, not that arbitrary service payloads were computed correctly or satisfy a business objective.
